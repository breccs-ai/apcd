Business Connect

Reach Donegal's African community through APCD.

Why Partner With Us

Access a Thriving, Engaged Community

APCD represents a growing and engaged African community across County Donegal. Our member organisations are trusted, active, and well-connected within their communities.

Whether you are a local business, service provider, healthcare organisation, training provider, or public body, connecting with APCD means connecting with a community that values trusted referrals and authentic engagement.

We do not charge for introductory contact. Reach out and tell us who you are and how you can serve our community.

Direct Community Access

Reach engaged African diaspora members across Donegal through trusted and established channels.

Authentic Partnerships

Work with a credible, constitutionally governed organisation with genuine community reach.

Events and Platform Presence

Gain visibility at APCD events including Africa Day and in community communications.

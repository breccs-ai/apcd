What We Do

Built on Four Pillars

Everything APCD does is rooted in four guiding principles that shape how we serve, advocate for, and celebrate African communities in Donegal.

Community

Bringing African families and individuals together across Donegal through shared identity, events, and support networks.

Culture

Celebrating and preserving the rich diversity of African cultures, traditions, languages, and heritage in Ireland.

Collaboration

Uniting our member organisations under one umbrella to speak with a single, powerful collective voice.

Empowerment

Supporting African community members to access services, opportunities, and representation in Donegal.

Our Mission

Amplifying African Voices in Donegal

APCD exists to provide a collective voice for African communities in Donegal. We work with statutory bodies, local councils, NGOs, and funders to ensure our communities are heard, represented, and served.

Cookie Policy

How and why we use cookies on this website, and how you can control them.

Last updated: May 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD) uses cookies and similar technologies on the website at www.apcdonegal.com. It also tells you how you can control or withdraw your consent to the use of cookies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, to improve the user experience, and to provide information to the site owner. Cookies can be set by the site you are visiting (known as first-party cookies) or by other parties whose content is loaded on the page (known as third-party cookies).

2. Cookies Used on This Website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. APCD does not use any advertising, marketing, or third-party analytics cookies. The only cookie set directly by this website is the one used to remember your consent preference for the cookie banner.

  • Strictly necessary cookies: a single first-party cookie named apcd_cookie_consent is set when you make a choice on the cookie banner. It stores either the value accepted or declined and expires automatically after twelve months. This cookie does not contain any personal data and is used solely to avoid showing the banner again on every page view.
  • Session storage: any session storage used by this website is not a cookie. It is cleared automatically when you close the browser tab, contains no personal data, and does not persist between sessions.
  • GitHub Pages: this website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. As a hosting provider, GitHub may set its own technical cookies and may collect basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type. APCD does not control these cookies or this collection. For full details please see the GitHub privacy statement at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement.

3. Cookies We Do Not Use

For the avoidance of doubt, APCD does not use any of the following on this website:

  • Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics service
  • Facebook Pixel or any other social media tracking pixel
  • Advertising networks of any kind
  • Remarketing or retargeting cookies
  • Third-party tracking scripts

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not track your activity across other websites.

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through the settings of your browser. Most browsers allow you to block all cookies or to accept only specific ones. The following links provide guidance for the most widely used browsers:

  • Google Chrome: support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Mozilla Firefox: support.mozilla.org/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences
  • Apple Safari: support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471
  • Microsoft Edge: support.microsoft.com/help/4027947

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites.

5. Your Consent

When you first visit this website you will see a cookie banner asking you to accept or decline the use of the consent cookie. Your choice is recorded in the apcd_cookie_consent cookie for a period of twelve months. You may withdraw or change your choice at any time by selecting the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page, which reopens the cookie banner.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect any change in our practices or in the law. The current version is identified by the date shown at the top of this page.

7. Contact

If you have any question about this Cookie Policy, please contact us at info@apcdonegal.com.

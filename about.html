About African People Collaboration Donegal

An umbrella organisation uniting African diaspora communities across County Donegal, Ireland.

Who We Are

One Organisation. Many Communities. One Voice.

African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD) is an umbrella community organisation representing the African diaspora living and working across County Donegal, Ireland.

Established to provide a unified structure for African community organisations in the region, APCD brings together a growing alliance of registered member groups under a single governance framework, enabling collective action, shared resources, and a stronger voice in public life.

We work with local councils, statutory bodies, community development organisations, and funders to ensure that African communities in Donegal are represented, supported, and empowered.

Our Values

What Guides Everything We Do

Inclusivity

Every African community member in Donegal deserves to feel seen, heard, and supported regardless of their background or country of origin.

Integrity

We operate with transparency, accountability, and respect for all member organisations and the people they represent.

Collaboration

Our strength comes from working together. No single group speaks for all. Every voice is valued.

Empowerment

We build capacity, create opportunities, and advocate for the communities we serve.

Our Governance

Structured for Growth and Accountability

APCD operates under a formal governance structure designed to ensure transparency, fairness, and long-term stability. Our Management Committee includes representation from all member organisations, with clear processes for decision-making, financial oversight, and conflict of interest management.

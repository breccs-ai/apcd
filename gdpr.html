GDPR and Data Protection

Our commitment to the General Data Protection Regulation and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

Last updated: May 2025

This page sets out how African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD) complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018. It complements our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy and should be read together with them.

1. Introduction

APCD is committed to upholding the data protection principles set out in the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. We process personal data lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner, and we only collect data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purposes for which it is collected.

2. Data Controller

The data controller for personal information collected through this website is African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD).

  • Name: African People Collaboration Donegal
  • Email: info@apcdonegal.com
  • Address: Letterkenny, County Donegal, Ireland

APCD is a small voluntary community organisation and does not carry out large-scale or systematic processing of personal data. We are therefore not required by the GDPR to appoint a Data Protection Officer. Responsibility for data protection matters is held collectively by the APCD Management Committee, and you may contact the committee on any data protection matter using the email address above.

3. Data We Collect and Why

We collect personal data through two forms on this website, and only where you choose to fill in those forms.

  • Contact form: we collect your name and email address, together with the subject and content of your message, so that we can respond to your enquiry.
  • Event RSVP form: we collect your name and email address, an optional phone number, the number of people attending, and any special requirements you choose to tell us about, so that we can manage attendance and plan for accessibility, catering, or other practical needs.

We do not collect any other categories of personal data through this website.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

Under Article 6 of the GDPR, every act of processing personal data must have a lawful basis. The bases on which APCD relies are as follows.

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): we rely on our legitimate interest as a community organisation in corresponding with people who contact us through our website and in managing attendance at community events.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a)): we rely on your consent for any optional information you choose to provide, including an optional phone number and any special requirements indicated on the event RSVP form. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR

The GDPR grants you a number of rights in relation to your personal data. These include:

  • The right of access to your personal data
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to erasure
  • The right to restriction of processing
  • The right to data portability
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@apcdonegal.com. In accordance with Article 12 of the GDPR, we will respond to your request without undue delay and in any event within one calendar month of receipt.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of twelve months from the date of the enquiry. Event RSVP submissions are retained until the event has taken place and for a maximum of six months thereafter. We do not retain personal data beyond these periods unless we are required to do so by law.

7. Data Security

APCD applies appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. This website is served exclusively over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and the hosting server. Forms on this website operate using front-end JavaScript and do not transmit your data to or store it on an APCD server.

8. Data Breaches

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of individuals, APCD will notify the Data Protection Commission of Ireland without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, in accordance with Article 33 of the GDPR. Where required by Article 34, we will also inform affected individuals directly and without undue delay.

9. Complaints

If you have a concern about how APCD has handled your personal data, please contact us first so that we have the opportunity to resolve the matter. You also have the right at any time to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland.

10. Updates to This Policy

This GDPR and Data Protection statement is reviewed at least once a year and is updated to reflect any change in our practices or in applicable law. The current version is identified by the date shown at the top of this page.

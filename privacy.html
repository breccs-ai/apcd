Privacy Policy

How African People Collaboration Donegal collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: May 2025

African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD) is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data in a transparent, lawful, and respectful manner. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, how we use it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over your data.

1. Who We Are

African People Collaboration Donegal (APCD) is a voluntary umbrella organisation based in Letterkenny, County Donegal, Ireland. APCD represents African community groups across the county and operates the website at www.apcdonegal.com.

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018, APCD is the data controller for any personal data submitted through this website. You can contact us at any time at info@apcdonegal.com.

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We only collect the personal data that you choose to provide to us through the forms on this website. We do not use cookies or scripts to collect personal data automatically beyond what is described in our Cookie Policy.

Through the contact form we collect the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • The subject of your message
  • The content of your message

Through the event RSVP form we collect the following information:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • An optional phone number
  • The number of people attending in your group
  • Any special requirements you choose to tell us about, for example accessibility or dietary needs

We do not collect any payment information through this website. We do not knowingly collect special category data (such as data revealing racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, or health information) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

3. How We Use Your Data

Personal data submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your enquiry and to follow up on any matter you raise with us. Personal data submitted through the event RSVP form is used only to manage attendance at the specific event you have registered for, including communicating event details to you and planning for any special requirements you have indicated.

We do not use your personal data for marketing purposes without your explicit consent. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal data with any commercial third party.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018. The lawful basis on which we rely is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and managing community events for our membership, together with your consent where you choose to provide optional information.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal data only for as long as is necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was provided.

Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of twelve months from the date of the enquiry, after which they are securely deleted. Event RSVP submissions are retained until the event has taken place and for a maximum of six months thereafter, to allow us to follow up on the event and produce a final attendance record, after which they are securely deleted.

6. Who Has Access to Your Data

Access to personal data submitted through this website is restricted to authorised members of the APCD Management Committee and to authorised volunteers acting on behalf of APCD. We do not share your personal data with any third-party organisations, advertisers, or commercial partners.

7. International Data Transfers

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc., a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation. As a result, some technical information about your visit (such as your IP address and browser type) may be processed by GitHub on servers located outside the European Economic Area. GitHub Pages operates in compliance with applicable data protection law and provides appropriate safeguards for international transfers. APCD does not transfer your personal data outside the European Economic Area for any other purpose.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have a number of rights in relation to the personal data we hold about you. These include:

  • The right of access to your personal data
  • The right to rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to erasure of your data (the right to be forgotten)
  • The right to object to processing
  • The right to restriction of processing
  • The right to data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@apcdonegal.com. If you believe we have not handled your personal data correctly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission of Ireland at www.dataprotection.ie.

9. Data Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS, which encrypts data in transit between your browser and the hosting server. The forms on this website operate entirely in your browser using front-end JavaScript. Submissions are not transmitted to or stored on any APCD server. Where a form invites you to contact us by email, your message is sent through your own email client, so the security of that message is governed by your email provider.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links to external websites operated by other organisations, including social media platforms. APCD is not responsible for the privacy practices of any external website and we encourage you to read the privacy policy of any external site that you visit.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in the law. The current version is identified by the date shown at the top of this page. Your continued use of this website following the publication of an updated policy constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

12. Contact

If you have any question about this Privacy Policy or about how APCD handles your personal data, please contact us:

Email: info@apcdonegal.com

Post: African People Collaboration Donegal, Letterkenny, County Donegal, Ireland.

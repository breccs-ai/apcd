APCD Registered Groups

The Organisations That Make APCD

APCD is made up of a growing number of registered member organisations, each serving distinct communities within the broader African diaspora in Donegal. Together we coordinate events, share resources, and collectively represent a community that continues to grow across the county.

Ghana Union Donegal (GUDL)

Representing the Ghanaian community in Donegal, promoting Ghanaian culture, solidarity, and community welfare across the county.

APCD Registered Member

African Community Donegal (ACD)

A broad-based organisation supporting African individuals and families in Donegal with integration, advocacy, and cultural programmes.

APCD Registered Member

New Community Networks (NCN)

Focused on building connections between new and established communities, supporting integration and social cohesion in Donegal.

APCD Registered Member

Letterkenny Sports and Social Club

Providing African community members with access to sports, recreation, and social activities in the Letterkenny area.

APCD Registered Member

Igbo Community NorthWest Ireland

Representing and celebrating the Igbo people of south-eastern Nigeria across the North West of Ireland, preserving language, culture, and community bonds.

APCD Registered Member

Sisters Doing It for Themselves

An empowerment network for African women in Donegal, building confidence, leadership, and mutual support among women in the diaspora.

APCD Registered Member
